---
title: "Dashboardr release party and workshop"
date: 2026-02-04
---


In February 2026, [Fabio Votta](https://www.uva.nl/en/profile/v/o/f.a.votta/f.a.votta.html), Alexandra Pafford, [Max Paulus](https://www.uva.nl/profiel/p/a/m.paulus/m.paulus.html), and [Ernesto de León](https://www.uva.nl/profiel/l/e/e.e.de-leon-williams/e.e.de-leon-williams.html) hosted a workshop and launch event aimed at faculty and researchers who want to communicate data and research findings through interactive dashboards, without requiring advanced computational skills. The event focused on how to use the dashboardr R package as part of a Digicomlab SeedFunding project to create clear, shareable dashboards with minimal code.
